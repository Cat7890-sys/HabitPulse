2. Information We Collect & Store Locally

HabitPulse is designed as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). The primary store for your habits, completion logs, streaks, and settings is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB and browser storage. This information remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to sign in and synchronize with cloud services.

3. Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore

If you choose to create an account or sign in using Google Sign-In or Email/Password, authentication and cloud synchronization are handled via Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (provided by Google Cloud / Firebase).

  • Authentication Data: Firebase processes basic authentication credentials such as your email address, display name, and authentication provider identifiers.
  • Cloud Firestore Data: When cloud synchronization is active, your habit definitions and completion logs are stored securely in your private user partition in Cloud Firestore.
  • Provider Processing: We rely on Firebase infrastructure. While security rules restrict data access to authorized users, provider-side processing and telemetry by Google have not undergone independent third-party audit beyond standard Google Cloud compliance terms.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The application may load font assets from Google Fonts or utilize hosting platforms (such as GitHub Pages). Standard web server logs collected by hosting infrastructure or third-party CDNs may record standard connection metadata (such as IP addresses and browser user-agent strings) governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Data Rights

You maintain full control over your local data. You can export your data as a JSON backup or permanently wipe all local records at any time via the application Settings menu. Account deletion requests regarding cloud records can be initiated through account management features or by contacting the operator placeholder above.
