2. Cookies vs. IndexedDB & Local Storage

It is important to distinguish traditional HTTP cookies from modern browser client-side storage technologies used by HabitPulse:

  • IndexedDB: HabitPulse primarily uses IndexedDB to store your local habit definitions, check-in logs, and streaks directly on your device for offline functionality.
  • Local Storage / Session Storage: May be used by authentication libraries (such as Firebase Auth) or UI preferences (such as dark mode settings).
  • Traditional Cookies: HabitPulse itself does not intentionally set proprietary marketing or tracking cookies. However, third-party infrastructure (such as Firebase authentication tokens or Google services) may place necessary functional cookies or tokens required for secure session management.

3. Analytics & Third-Party Tracking Verification Status

Any analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking mechanisms that may be present via integrated SDKs (such as Firebase or hosting providers) require further verification and complete technical audit. We do not maintain an exhaustive pre-verified inventory of every third-party request emitted by underlying platform libraries.

4. Managing Your Browser Storage

You can clear local data, IndexedDB records, and storage cache at any time through your browser settings or via the application reset options in the HabitPulse settings menu. Note that clearing browser storage will remove offline local records that have not been synchronized to the cloud.
